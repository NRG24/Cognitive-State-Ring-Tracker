Why NERVA

Wellness wearables watch your body.
NERVA listens to your nervous system.

Cognitive-first

Tuned strictly for mental wellness — stress, arousal, and mental fatigue — instead of gym reps and step counts.

Clean signals

A dedicated 6-axis IMU detects and filters motion noise from raw biometric streams before it ever reaches your score.

Validated hardware

Every circuit modeled and verified in LT-Spice, then squeezed onto a circular 2-layer flex PCB assembled by JLCPCB.

Real-time telemetry

Low-latency Bluetooth Low Energy streams six live metrics straight to your phone with a tiny power budget.

Smart pipeline

In-app filtering removes artifacts, then a custom algorithm distills everything into a Cognitive Score out of 100.

Fully open

Firmware, Flutter app, KiCad schematics, and the full bill of materials — all open-source and on GitHub.

The Cognitive Score

One number for how your mind is holding up

NERVA fuses your live biometrics into a single score out of 100. Under the hood the algorithm weighs three qualities of your electrodermal signal — then layers in heart-rate variability and context from the IMU.

  • Variability40%moment-to-moment change in skin conductance
  • Trend30%direction of arousal over the session
  • Stability30%how steady your baseline stays

Paired with activity recognition, stress-pattern detection, and trigger identification, the score becomes a picture of when — and why — your mind runs hot.
